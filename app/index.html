The one rule that saves runs

GTA VI has no mission replay. Keep a rotating manual save at the start of every chapter, plus one right before the final mission. That save file is the only way back if you miss the Chapter 1 meditation spot, want to see the other side of a story choice, or need to retry a one-off moment. Autosave alone is not enough.
