    Where you stand

    PowerPyx flags one trophy as possibly missable — and only as a maybe. Everything else on the list is safe: story trophies cannot be missed, and the whole 100% checklist stays open in free-roam after the story.

    Why the rest is safe

    • There is free-roam after the story, but no mission replay — so keep a save at the start and end of each chapter (15 manual save slots).
    • Optional missions and quest givers that vanish for a while come back later; every trophy-related character is available again after the story.
    • Cheats disable trophies and saving — keep trophy runs cheat-free (cheats only unlock after the credits anyway).
    • Some magazine collectibles sit in story buildings that later close, but they also spawn elsewhere and are not needed for any trophy.

    Source: PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap.

    How this guide was built

    • The list is official. All 55 names, descriptions and grades are as PlayStation publishes them, via Exophase and PSNProfiles — the two agree on every name, description and grade. 28 of the 55 are hidden on PlayStation until earned.
    • The guides are our own words. Each trophy's how-to is written for this guide from the official description and from PowerPyx's trophy guide, and names its sources beside it. Nothing is copied from another guide. Five trophies are still unconfirmed at launch — this guide flags them as Unconfirmed instead of inventing steps.
    • It works offline. Every file lives in this folder; the app fetches nothing at run time. Your ticks, counters and notes are saved in this browser only (localStorage) and can be exported to a file and imported back — or wiped with Reset.
    • No Rockstar art. The look is our own — no logo, box art or game screenshot anywhere in this guide.
